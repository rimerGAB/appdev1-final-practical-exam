# APPDEV1 Final Practical Exam

## Reflection

At first, I just wanted to finish fast because we only had a limited time. But while working on this practical exam, I realized that I needed to slow down a little and check the instructions one by one. There were times when I had to ask why something changed or why nothing seemed to change. Checking each part before moving on helped me understand the project better.

One example was removing the Name field from the Login page. I thought something was missing at first, but it was actually part of the instructions. Another was replacing the regular inputs with shadcn/ui Input. The page still looked the same, so I thought nothing changed. I learned that a change in the code does not always mean there will be a visible change on the screen.

I also learned to pay more attention to Git commits, screenshots, and filenames. It was not just about getting the page to look right. I had to make sure the changes and screenshots were included in the correct commit. Using CSS modules and design tokens also helped me see how styles can be organized and reused between the Sign up and login pages.

Also, using AI as a tool made this practical exam smoother. It guided me through the steps, gave me explanations and coding help, and helped me organize my screenshots and commits. This helped me be more productive, but I also learned that I should ask questions instead of just accepting every suggestion. When something seemed different from the instructions, I asked about it and checked what needed to change. For me, the useful part was being able to ask why something worked that way and get an explanation while working on the project.

Overall, this exam showed me the importance of checking my work instead of rushing through everything. I still need more practice with React, Git, and styling, but I understand the steps better now. Next time, I want to be more organized from the start so I can spend less time correcting things and more time understanding what I am doing.
